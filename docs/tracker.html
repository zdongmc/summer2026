Printable Reading Tracker

Color in a book spine for every book. A circle for every reading day.

Home Printable Tracker
How to use: Print this page (landscape works best). Your child writes the book title on each spine and colors it in when they finish. Color a circle for each day they read — hitting 20 days completes the FCPL challenge. Reaching book 12 completes the White House challenge.

Set your printer to landscape orientation for best results.
